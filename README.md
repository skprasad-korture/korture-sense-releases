# Korture Sense for Mac

Korture Sense measures how you work with AI, helps you understand the patterns, and builds an AI setup around how you want to improve. It runs on your own Mac and reads your own AI chats with the AI you already use. Nothing leaves your Mac.

This repository holds only the finished app, signed by S K Prasad (KK5JPU56Y2) and notarized by Apple. Download the newest `Korture-Sense-arm64.zip` from [Releases](../../releases/latest), unzip it, and move Korture Sense to your Applications folder. The app keeps itself up to date from here.

More at [korture.ai](https://korture.ai).
